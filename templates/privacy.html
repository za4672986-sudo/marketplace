Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ now.strftime('%Y-%m-%d') }} · TradeLink Wholesale

1. What we collect

Account data (name, business name, email, phone), addresses you save, orders and payment records (payment method and reference only — never card or wallet credentials), quotes, messages, wishlists, and basic usage data (pages visited, search terms) for performance and security.

2. How we use it

To operate your account, fulfil orders, confirm payments, send order notifications (by in-app notification and, when configured, email), prevent fraud, and improve the marketplace. We do not sell personal data.

3. Sharing

Order and messaging data is shared with the Supplier (or Buyer) on the other side of a transaction, and with payment providers strictly to process your chosen payment. Data may be shared where required by law.

4. Security

Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 (120,000 iterations); sessions are signed; payment credentials never touch our servers. No system is 100% secure, but we follow industry practice and review our controls regularly.

5. Retention

We retain account and order records for as long as your account is active and for the periods required by tax and accounting law (typically 6–7 years for transaction records).

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, or close your account, by contacting us. We respond within 30 days. Deleting your account does not delete transaction records required by law.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: contact us or email {{ merchant_email }}.
