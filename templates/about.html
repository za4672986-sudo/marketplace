About TradeLink

The wholesale marketplace built for serious buyers and suppliers

TradeLink connects verified manufacturers and wholesalers with retailers, distributors, and growing businesses — with transparent tiered pricing, bulk quoting, and in-platform ordering.

Our mission

Sourcing wholesale inventory should be as simple as buying retail. We remove the guesswork: every supplier on TradeLink is identity-checked and business-verified, every product carries clear MOQs and tiered pricing, and every order is tracked from placement to delivery. Buyers get market-benchmarked pricing — including one-click comparison against Alibaba, DHGate, and Made-in-China — while suppliers get direct access to qualified, order-ready buyers.
