Returns & Refunds

Last updated: {{ now.strftime('%Y-%m-%d') }} · TradeLink Wholesale

1. Wholesale orders

Because orders are business-to-business and often produced to order, standard wholesale purchases are non-refundable once they enter production. Your quote and order documents state production and delivery terms; please review them before paying.

2. When you can get a refund

• Order cancelled by the supplier before shipment — full refund.
• Items received damaged, defective, or not matching the agreed specification — refund or replacement at your choice, within 14 days of delivery.
• Payment made but order not fulfilled within the agreed delivery window (excluding force majeure) — full refund.
• Incorrectly charged amount — refund of the difference.

3. How to request one

Message the supplier from your order page with your order number, photos of any issue, and the outcome you want (refund/replacement). If the supplier does not respond within 5 business days, escalate via contact form and we will review the case.

4. Refund timing

Approved refunds are returned via the original payment channel within 10 business days of approval. Wallet and mobile-money refunds (EasyPaisa, JazzCash, bKash, GCash, M-PESA) are typically visible within 24–72 hours depending on the provider.

5. Disputes

If a dispute can't be resolved between buyer and supplier, TradeLink's marketplace team will arbitrate using order records, messages, and delivery evidence. Our decision is final except where law provides otherwise.
